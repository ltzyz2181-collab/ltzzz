Privacy Policy

LTZZZ AI Content Automation (the "App"), operated by LTZZZ Digital Lab (the "Operator"), effective 2026-10-01.

1. Scope

This policy describes how the App handles information when you use its TikTok integration (Content Posting API) to schedule and publish original AI-assisted videos to TikTok.

2. Data We Access

The App accesses, only with your explicit OAuth authorization:

- user.info.basic: your TikTok username and basic public profile, used to identify your account for publishing.

- video.upload: used solely to upload videos you designate, on your schedule, to your own TikTok account.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

- We do not collect, store, or share your private messages, contacts, location, device identifiers, or payment information.

- We do not read or analyze your TikTok followers, DMs, or private content.

- We do not sell or rent any user data to third parties.

4. Storage & Security

- OAuth tokens are stored encrypted as Cloudflare Worker secrets / KV, never in public repositories.

- Access to tokens is restricted to the publishing worker via signed requests.

- You can revoke access at any time via your TikTok developer dashboard or by contacting us.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

- Publishing logs (video ID, title, publish time, status) are retained for operational audit and are listed on our public dashboard.

- To request deletion of any stored record, contact us; deletion is completed within 30 days.

6. Third-Party Services

The App uses TikTok's official Content Posting API, Cloudflare Workers (execution), and Cloudflare KV (token storage). These services process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Changes & Contact

We may update this policy and will note the effective date above. Questions: ltzyz2181@gmail.com.
